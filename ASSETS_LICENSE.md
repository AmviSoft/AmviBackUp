## English

The source code of AmviBackUp is released under the MIT License (see `LICENSE`).
The MIT License does **not** cover the items below. They are included only so that the
official program works, and all rights to them are reserved by the author:

- the photographs in `assets/support/` (`dog_*.jpg`);
- the payment QR codes and receiving addresses in `assets/support/` and in `amvibackup_support.py`;
- the name "AmviBackUp" and the application icon.

You may redistribute these items only as part of unmodified official AmviBackUp releases.

together with a SHA-256 checksum.

Лицензия MIT **не распространяется** на следующее. Эти материалы включены только для работы

Распространять их можно только в составе неизменённых официальных выпусков AmviBackUp.
